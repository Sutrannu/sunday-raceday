Forza Horizon 6 -- Getting Money AFK -- Colossus Rivals

In FH6 we now have the Auto Driving Feature through ANNA. We can use
that to AFK Race the Colossus against a Rival that is much better than
us (so we can't win) for hours.

And you don't even have to have unlocked The Colossus race yet. :)

Ideally you should have the [Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro Forza Edition](https://www.kudosprime.com/fh6/car_sheet.php?id=590)
, since that gives you a Credits Boost. Other cars work as well,
but you get less money.

Probably good to grab some tune that has "AFK" in the name.

Note: For Heat and Power consumption it is probably good to lower your
graphics settings so the PC/Console is less stressed doing this.

So how do we do this?

1. On the XBox, use a wired controller. Open Settings ➡️
   Preferences ➡️ Idle Options, and uncheck "Show me
   things when idle".
1.  In the Main Menu go to ONLINE ➡️ RIVALS ➡️ ROAD RACING
   ![Online](../images/colossus-money-01.png)
   ![RIVALS](../images/colossus-money-02.png)
   ![ROAD RACING](../images/colossus-money-03.png)
1.  Choose \"The Colossus\" (navigate 2 steps to the left)
   ![The Colossus](../images/colossus-money-04.png)
1.  Press "Y" and choose the fastest Rival.
1.  Choose R Class or whatever the car and tune of your choice is in.
   ![Choose R Class](../images/colossus-money-05.png)
1.  Choose your car.
   ![Choose your car](../images/colossus-money-06.png)
1.  Go to \"Difficulty & Settings\"
   ![Difficulty & Settings](../images/colossus-money-07.png)
1.  Set your assists (Drivatar Difficulty doesn\'t matter here) to
    maximize Bonus Credits (Bonus CR).
   ![Set assists](../images/colossus-money-08.png)
1.  If needed, set your controls so you can activate ANNA
   ![Set your controls](../images/colossus-money-09.png)
   ![Set your controls](../images/colossus-money-10.png)
1.  Start the Race, then activate ANNA and choose \"Enable AUTO-DRIVE\"
   ![Enable AUTO-DRIVE](../images/colossus-money-11.png)
1.  The game will tell you the lap is invalidated, but thats okay. We
    don\'t want to beat the rival anyway.
   ![invalidated](../images/colossus-money-12.png)
1. Let it drive for as long as you want it to.
1. When you wanna stop, go to the Menu and "Quit Event".
   ![Quit Event](../images/colossus-money-13.png)

In my test I got over 3 million credits in 2.5hrs. Your mileage may vary. :)
