## How to play

Learn how to reset your car.
Usually, **Pause Menu** => **X** => **A**

Fast travel to the Estate in mid-upper left of the map.
Find the entrance to the stadium. Hando will dive to the staring spot, and call the ball in play.
Push him off the platform.

You must keep moving, or stay behind the starting zone line,
for the ball to be in play. Something likke that. We're still
making this up.

When things get heated, FH will start ghosting cars, even Hando. Just remember, none of this is fair, or makes any sense.

## Making a Stadium in FH6

September 20, 2016. Based on "Handoball Alpha 0.0.2" ShareCode `865.117.170`

To play Handoball in FH6, you need a stadium with the following.

- A platform to push Hando off
- A moat around the platform that can contain all cars that get pushed off. Preferably without getting in the way of all the cars racing off at high speed.
- The bottom of the moat has to be non-spawnable (not NavMesh, unwalkable), forcing the player to reset or rewind back to the platform
- A starting marker in the center of the platform for Hando to park on
- (optional) A boundary marker around the starting marker for the player to stay behind

Stadiums need a ramp to enter from above.
It's unlikely to completly prevent glitches in the moat, ever. Cars will ghost, and players will need to fast-travel back to the estate. So it's recommended to place the stadium near the Estate entrance, with enough room for the entrance ramp.

Important: The bottom of the moat could be rocks, walls, fences, etc. Anything unbreakable that will not set the spawn point. The car must remain unstable so thaat resetting will return the player to the platform.

The CarPark: Middle Cladding prop covers the most lateral space, plenty for the width of a moat, and can be placed repeatedly while strafing. The spacing needs to be close enough to prevent smaller cars from sliding between, upright.

Based on testing; the various platform props are the best, easiest, and fastest for the platform, and the inner & outer walls of moat.
